What We Offer

Complete Eye Care
Under One Roof

Our experienced team provides a full spectrum of vision and eye health services for the whole Bristol community.

Private Eye Exams
Advanced assessment using OCT, Biometric Eye Scanning (DNEye Scanner) and antioxidant screening.
Sustainable & Designer Eyewear
A carefully curated collection of frames for all ages and budgets — from everyday essentials to statement designer pieces fitted with precision.
Contact Lens Fitting
Full contact lens fitting service including complex prescriptions, specialist lenses for dry eye, keratoconus, and myopia control.
Myopia Management
Specialist assessment and treatment for short-sightedness in children and adults. Using the latest evidence-based techniques to slow myopia progression and protect long-term vision.
Dry Eye Clinic
Specialist assessment and management of dry eye syndrome using the latest diagnostic tools and clinically proven treatment plans tailored to your needs.
Lumecca-I
Intense Pulsed Light (IPL) therapy to reduce vascular inflammation and treat dry eye and ocular surface disease. Delivers precise, targeted spot-size treatments for effective, lasting relief.
Forma-I
Bipolar Radiofrequency (RF) therapy delivering targeted energy to the delicate periorbital area and meibomian glands. Improves meibum quality for dry eye relief whilst stimulating collagen production to rejuvenate the periorbital area.
Heart.Eye Consultation
Eye-based Cardiovascular risk screening with Heart Eye by Dr Noon
Our Practice

Local, Independent
& Dedicated to You

Clifton Vision was created by Sal Jaffe to bring together advanced optometry, specialist dry eye care and a more personal approach to eye health.

Every consultation is designed to understand the individual behind the prescription — from detailed eye health assessment and contact lenses to dry eye diagnostics, myopia control and Heart Eye screening.

  • Advanced Private eye examinations for all ages
  • Experienced optometrists and dry eye specialists
  • Contact Lens specialist
  • Wide range of frames to suit every budget
  • Eyecare Plan — affordable monthly access to full eyecare (from £13.50)
  • Specialist clinics for dry eye, myopia control & more
  • A welcoming, patient-first environment
