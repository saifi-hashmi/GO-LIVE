Dry Eye Clinic

Comfortable eyes. Better days.

A detailed dry eye consultation designed to understand why your eyes feel dry, gritty, watery, tired or irritated — and what can be done.

Your consultation

We combine symptoms, clinical examination and imaging to build a picture of your tear film, eyelids and meibomian glands.

Imaging

Observ 520x

Advanced external eye and skin analysis to help assess visible signs around the eyelids and ocular surface.

Tear film

Schirmer test

Measures tear production and helps assess the aqueous component of the tear film.

Ocular surface

Fluorescein test

A diagnostic dye is used to assess how the tear-film behaves across the surface of the eye and whether there are areas the tears aren't protecting.

Clinical exam

Slit lamp examination

Detailed examination of the eyelids, lashes, tear film, meibomian glands, conjunctiva and cornea under magnification.

Myopia Control

Helping children see clearly — now and into the future.

We offer specialist options designed to help manage myopia progression in children and young people.

What is myopia?

When distant vision becomes blurred.

Myopia is a condition where eyes grow too long, causing blurry distance vision. This can make someone very reliant on vision correction and put them more at risk of eye disease in the future.

Heart Eye screening

Look beyond vision.

The eye contains a unique network of tiny blood vessels. Heart Eye screening uses retinal information as part of a wider assessment of cardiovascular health and risk.

At Clifton Vision, we can discuss whether Heart Eye screening is appropriate for you and explain your results in the context of your wider eye examination.
