Who We Are

Clifton Vision is an independent optician practice located at 174 Whiteladies Road, Clifton, Bristol. We provide professional eye care services, dispensing of spectacles and contact lenses, and related optical products.

For the purposes of data protection law, Clifton Vision is the data controller of the personal information we collect about you.

Contact us:

Post: Clifton Vision, 174 Whiteladies Road, Clifton, Bristol

Email: info@cliftonvision.co.uk

02

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly
  • Name, date of birth, address, telephone number and email address
  • Optical and health records, including prescription details, ocular health history and general health information relevant to your eye care
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communications you send to us, including enquiries submitted via our website contact form or interest-registration form
Information collected automatically
  • Website usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies, including pages visited, time spent on pages and referring URLs
  • Device and browser information (browser type, operating system, IP address)
Information from third parties
  • Referral information from other healthcare providers (e.g. your GP or hospital ophthalmologist) where clinically relevant
03

How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

To provide eye care services (Contract / Legitimate Interest)
  • Creating and maintaining your clinical record
  • Carrying out eye examinations and issuing prescriptions
  • Dispensing spectacles, contact lenses and other optical appliances
  • Recalling you for routine appointments and follow-up care
To communicate with you (Consent / Legitimate Interest)
  • Responding to website enquiries and contact form submissions
  • Sending appointment reminders and recall notices
  • Sending promotional information about our services, offers and new products where you have given consent
To comply with legal obligations (Legal Obligation)
  • Retaining clinical records for the minimum periods required by NHS and professional guidelines
  • Responding to lawful requests from regulatory or law-enforcement bodies
To improve our practice (Legitimate Interest)
  • Analysing anonymised website and appointment data to improve our services
  • Managing the security and functionality of our website
04

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Healthcare professionals involved in your care (e.g. GPs, hospital eye departments) where clinically necessary
  • Our optical suppliers and laboratories, solely to fulfil your order
  • IT and software service providers who support our practice management and website systems, under appropriate data processing agreements
  • EmailJS, our website form delivery service used to route enquiries to our inbox
  • Regulatory bodies (e.g. the General Optical Council) or law-enforcement agencies where we are legally required to do so

All third parties are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

05

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience. Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We use:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g. pages viewed, session duration)

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

06

Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, and in accordance with professional and legal obligations:

  • Adult clinical records: a minimum of 10 years from the date of last entry (in line with General Optical Council guidance)
  • Children's clinical records: retained until the patient reaches age 25, or 10 years from the date of last entry, whichever is longer
  • Website enquiry data: up to 2 years, unless you ask us to remove it sooner
  • Financial records: 6 years in accordance with HMRC requirements

After the applicable retention period, data is securely destroyed.

07

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@cliftonvision.co.uk. We will respond within one calendar month. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

08

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction or damage. These include secure storage systems, restricted access controls and encrypted communications where appropriate.

Whilst we take these measures seriously, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. If you have concerns about the security of information you have shared with us, please contact us immediately.

09

Children's Privacy

We provide eye care services to patients of all ages. Where a patient is under 16, we will seek consent from a parent or guardian before collecting sensitive health information.

Our website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 via our website contact forms.

10

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and recommend you review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available on our website and in our practice. The effective date at the top of this document will reflect the date of the most recent revision.

For material changes, we will notify you by email (if you are a registered patient) or by a prominent notice on our website.
